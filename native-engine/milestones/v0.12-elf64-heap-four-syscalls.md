## Previously observed on REDMAGIC device

User-provided physical Android screenshots establish app-reported success
of genuine signed-APK-embedded FEXCore x86-64 ELF64 execution in v0.10,
followed by copying immutable ELF `.rodata` into writable ELF `.data`

## Two integrated development stages

**Stage 1: bounded guest heap**:
`native-engine/android-host/kernix_guest_memory_syscalls.h`
owns exactly one 16-KiB, RW, anonymous, private Android Bionic `mmap`
region. Strictly reject nonzero requested address, wrong guest size,
executable permissions, writable/executable combined pages, file-backed
mapping, a second overlapping allocation, bad message address/length and
nonmatching munmap arguments. A disposable Android child reclaims memory
on all exits. No root or direct Android external program loading.

**Stage 2: ordered Linux x86-64 syscall dispatch**:
The separately GNU-as/ld-built x86-64 PIE
`native-engine/android-guest/kernix_elf64_heap_syscalls_guest.S` issues
exactly `mmap(9)`, `write(1)`, `getpid(39)`, `munmap(11)`.
The callback checks four exact instruction offsets/RIP/RCX:
`33/35`, `73/75`, `82/84`, `100/102`.
The guest allocates 16KiB, copies a fixed 26-byte message from its
read-only ELF PT_LOAD into that newly allocated memory, writes exactly
26 bytes to stdout, gets its genuine Android host PID, unmaps the
allocation, then computes final x86 RAX=PID+26 before HLT.

This is a **sample-only four-syscall bridge**; it is **not** a full Linux
syscall ABI or general guest `mmap` policy. The ELF loader
`native-engine/android-host/kernix_elf64_loader.h` adds a third
pinned, no-relocation, W^X load-layout profile while retaining v0.10
and v0.11 profiles unmodified for regression.
